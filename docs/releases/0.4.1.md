## Fixed and improved
- **Containers have a network on Linux hosts where Docker Engine ran, or with ufw or firewalld.** Docker leaves the iptables FORWARD policy at DROP, even after it stops, and every forward hook must accept a packet, so Kiri's own nftables rules were not enough: containers resolved names but could reach nothing. Kiri now adds an accept for its bridges (`kiri+`, marked with the comment `kiri`) to Docker's `DOCKER-USER` chain, or to the top of `FORWARD` when its policy drops, and removes it when the engine stops. Kiri's own forward rules still keep the LAN away from containers.
